SURFACE by MONARO
Installation guide


macOS (11 or later, VST3 and Audio Unit)
----------------------------------------
The installer is not notarised by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time.
This is expected. Follow these steps once:

1. Download Surface_Installer.pkg and double-click it.
2. macOS says it cannot open the file. Click Done (or OK).
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
4. Scroll down to the Security section and click Open Anyway next to
   Surface_Installer.pkg. Enter your password if asked.
5. Click Open Anyway in the window that appears. The installer starts.
6. Follow the installer, then restart your DAW.

The plug-ins go to:
  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components


Windows (10 or 11, 64-bit, VST3)
--------------------------------
The installer is not code-signed, so Windows may warn about it.

1. Download Surface_Installer.exe and double-click it.
2. If SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click More info,
   then Run anyway.
3. Click Yes when Windows asks for administrator permission.
4. Follow the installer, then restart your DAW.

The plug-in goes to:
  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3


Activate your license
---------------------
1. Insert Surface on a track in your DAW and open its window.
2. Enter the key from your purchase message in the activation window.
   Your computer needs internet access for this step.

Your key comes from the Telegram bot @monarolab_bot right after payment.
Without a key, Surface runs as a 7-day trial on each computer.
A license covers one computer.


Surface is not in your DAW?
---------------------------
- Rescan plug-ins in your DAW.
- On macOS, check that the formats were selected in the installer.
- On Windows, make sure your DAW scans the VST3 folder above.
- Pro Tools is not supported: Surface has no AAX version.
